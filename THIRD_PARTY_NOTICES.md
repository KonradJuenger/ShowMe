# Third-party notices

## Material Design Icons

The icon paths in `src/components/MaterialIcon.tsx` and the application icon
in `public/` and `build/` use Material Design Icons from
[Pictogrammers](https://github.com/Templarian/MaterialDesign).
The upstream notice is retained in `src/assets/material-icons/LICENSE`.
The Apache License 2.0 text is in `src/assets/material-icons/APACHE-2.0.txt`.

## Package dependencies

Runtime and build dependencies are installed through `package.json` and
`package-lock.json` and retain their upstream licenses. Local packaged builds
include the dependency and Electron license notices supplied by those packages.
